A Buyer's Guide to Sourcing Premium Frozen Seafood in the UAE

What to look for when selecting a frozen seafood supplier — from IQF standards to cold-chain compliance.

The UAE seafood market exceeds AED 2 billion annually, driven by a diverse population with appetites spanning Arabic grills, Asian stir-fries and European fine dining. Frozen seafood plays a critical role in meeting this demand year-round.

What defines premium frozen seafood?

  • Blast freezing at source: Fish frozen within hours of catch retain texture and flavor far better than slow-frozen alternatives.
  • IQF technology: Individual Quick Freezing prevents clumping and allows precise portion control.
  • Glazing standards: Reputable suppliers maintain glazing below 10% — excess ice means you're paying for water.
  • Cold-chain integrity: From container to warehouse to delivery truck, temperatures must stay below -18°C.
