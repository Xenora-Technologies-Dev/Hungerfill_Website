How to Choose the Right B2B Food Supplier in Dubai

Seven criteria every restaurant, hotel and retailer should evaluate before signing with a food trading partner.

Choosing a B2B food supplier is one of the most consequential decisions a food business makes. The right partner ensures consistent quality, competitive pricing and reliable delivery. The wrong one leads to stockouts, quality complaints and wasted margin.

Seven evaluation criteria

  1. Product range: Can they supply your full basket — seafood, poultry, meat, eggs and dry goods?
  2. Cold-chain capability: Do they own or control temperature-controlled storage and delivery?
  3. Certifications: Halal and municipality approvals should be current and verifiable.
  4. Delivery reliability: Ask for references and check delivery track records.
  5. Pricing transparency: Clear invoicing without hidden fees or inconsistent weights.
  6. Responsiveness: Can you reach a sales manager quickly when you need an urgent order?
  7. Flexibility: Will they accommodate custom cuts, pack sizes and mixed-container orders?
