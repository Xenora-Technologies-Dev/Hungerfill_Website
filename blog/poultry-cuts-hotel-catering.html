Top Poultry Cuts for Hotel & Catering Operations

From breast fillets to leg quarters — choosing the right chicken cut can reduce food cost and kitchen labor.

Chicken is the most consumed protein in the UAE foodservice sector. For hotel and catering buyers, selecting the right cut impacts food cost, prep time and menu versatility.

High-demand cuts

  • Breast fillets: The all-rounder for grills, salads, sandwiches and shredded applications. IQF portions eliminate thawing waste.
  • Leg quarters: Best value for BBQ buffets, fried chicken and cafeteria service.
  • Whole chicken: Ideal for rotisserie, stuffed roasts and traditional roastery counters.
