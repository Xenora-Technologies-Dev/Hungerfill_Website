The UAE's diverse population creates strong demand for authentic Indian mutton (goat) and beef cuts alongside local and imported lamb. Hungerfill Foods specialises in fresh and frozen Indian mutton carcass and a full range of frozen Indian beef cuts — halal certified and handled with care from abattoir to delivery.

From traditional biryanis and karahi to stew cuts and mince for kebabs, our meat products serve butcheries, hotels and restaurants that demand authentic flavour profiles and consistent quality.

Channels & Opportunities in Dubai & the GCC

  • Butcheries & Meat Shops: Whole and half carcass for in-store cutting; popular in areas with strong South Asian and Arab communities.
  • Hotels & Restaurants: Mutton for Arabic haneeth, Yemeni mandi and South Asian curries; beef cubes and mince for grills and stews.
  • Catering & Events: Bulk mutton and beef for weddings, Ramadan iftar spreads and large-scale catering in Dubai and Sharjah.
  • Retail Chains: Pre-cut frozen portions for supermarket meat sections serving expatriate communities.
  • GCC Distribution: Dubai as a hub for meat re-export to Oman, Bahrain and Saudi markets with growing demand for halal Indian-origin meat.
